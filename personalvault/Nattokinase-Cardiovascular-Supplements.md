## Summary of the Thread
The thread promotes **nattokinase** (fermented soybean enzyme from natto) as a natural fibrinolytic that:
- Dissolves blood clots (breaks down fibrin, up to 4x more effective than plasmin in some claims)
- Lowers blood pressure (via ACE-inhibitor-like pathway)
- Reduces arterial plaque (citing ~36% reduction in studies)
- Lowers clotting factors (fibrinogen, Factor VII/VIII)
- Improves circulation, optimizes cholesterol, and may protect against cognitive decline

**Recommended dose in thread:** ≥8,000 FU/day on empty stomach. Includes affiliate link to a specific 8,000 FU product.

**Safety note in thread:** Caution with blood thinners (including aspirin). Consult doctor.

## Evidence Check (Real Studies)
- **Plaque reduction (36% claim):** Supported by:
  - 2017 RCT (n=76): 6,000 FU/day for 26 weeks → **36.6%** carotid plaque reduction (vs 11.5% with simvastatin). Also better IMT improvement. Plaque benefit independent of lipid changes.
  - 2022 study (n=1,062): **10,800 FU/day** for 12 months → ~**36%** carotid plaque area reduction + IMT improvements. Dose-dependent; lower doses (3,600 FU) ineffective.
- **Blood pressure:** Supported by 2023 meta-analysis of RCTs (systolic ↓ ~3.45 mmHg, diastolic ↓ ~2.32 mmHg). Earlier trials confirm modest benefits in pre-hypertensives/hypertensives.
- **Clot-dissolving:** Strong mechanistic + human data (2015 crossover study: single 2,000 FU dose increased D-dimer/FDP, prolonged clotting times, reduced Factor VIII within hours).
- **Overall:** Promising adjunct for mild atherosclerosis, BP, and fibrinolysis at higher doses (6,000–10,800 FU). Not a statin replacement. Mostly carotid plaque data; more outcome studies needed.

**Caveats:** Promotional tone (health coach selling coaching/supplements). Always medical supervision, especially with meds or bleeding risk.

## Other Good Supplements for Cardiovascular Health / Plaque / BP Support
These have solid evidence as adjuncts (pair with training, diet, sleep via SwolTracker):

- **Omega-3 Fish Oil (EPA/DHA, 2–4g/day combined)**: Strong evidence for lowering triglycerides, modest BP reduction, anti-inflammatory, heart health. Top-tier recommendation.
- **Vitamin D3 + K2 (MK-7)**: D3 for overall health; K2 helps direct calcium to bones instead of arteries (arterial calcification support). Common stack with nattokinase.
- **Magnesium (glycinate or taurate, 300–400mg elemental)**: Excellent for BP lowering, vascular relaxation, sleep/recovery. Highly relevant for training.
- **CoQ10 (100–200mg ubiquinol)**: Supports heart energy, BP, and counters statin side effects if applicable.
- **Berberine (500mg 2–3x/day)**: Improves lipids, insulin sensitivity, modest BP effects. Good metabolic stack.
- **Garlic Extract (standardized allicin, 600–1,200mg)**: Modest BP and lipid benefits.
- **Beetroot Powder / Nitrates or L-Arginine**: Boost nitric oxide for better blood flow/BP.
