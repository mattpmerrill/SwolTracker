## Description (4000 chars max)

SwolTracker is the strength training app that actually thinks about your gains.

Tell it your goals, your schedule, and the equipment you have access to — then let the AI build you a personalized 4-week lifting program. Every week, it adjusts. Every set, it tracks.

**SMART PROGRAMMING**
Stop guessing what to lift. SwolTracker's AI generates weekly workout programs based on your 1-rep maxes, fitness goals, available equipment, and training history. Programs are percentage-based — every weight is calculated automatically as you get stronger.

**EFFORTLESS TRACKING**
Tap to log each set. See your prescribed weight vs. what you actually lifted. Built-in rest timer starts automatically between sets, so you never lose track of your rest periods.

**KNOW YOUR NUMBERS**
Track all your 1-rep maxes in one place. Watch your strength levels visualize across your lifts. See weekly volume charts and historical strength curves to understand exactly how you're progressing.

**TRAIN WITH YOUR CREW**
Create a training group with friends, family, or your gym squad. Follow the same program, cheer each other on, and keep each other accountable.

**APPLE HEALTH SYNC**
SwolTracker syncs your completed workouts directly to Apple Health, including duration and estimated calories burned. It can also read your body weight to fine-tune your program recommendations.

**WORKS OFFLINE**
Every workout is stored locally first. Train underground, in the mountains, wherever — your data syncs when you're back online.

---

**THE DETAILS**
- Percentage-based programming (e.g., 75% of your squat 1RM)
- AI regenerates your program weekly based on performance
- Set-by-set logging with haptic feedback
- Automatic rest timer with adjustable duration
- 1RM tracker with progression charts
- Weekly volume bar charts
- Buddy system with crew/group training
- Offline-first with background sync
- Apple Health integration
